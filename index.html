Explore

Every coffee & matcha spot near you

Browse a map of local shops, filter for WiFi, outlets or local spots only, and see how many people are there right now.

Check in

Visit cards that fill up as you go

Check in when you're at a shop and get a stamp. Ten stamps fill a card, and your levels, streaks and passport grow with every visit.

Shop chats

Ask the people who are there

Every shop has its own chat. Is it busy? Good for studying? Is the seasonal drink worth it? Messages disappear after 24 hours, and you can chat under an alias.

Sip with me

Grab a coffee with friends

Invite your friends to meet up now or later, and see who's in. Get a heads-up when a friend checks in nearby, only if they choose to share it.

Every Sunday

Your week in coffee

New spots you tried, your go-to, first sips and where your friends went, all in one recap worked out right on your phone.

Privacy-first
