Help & contact

Questions, a problem with your account, or something to report? Email support@letssip.app. We reply within 2 business days and review reports of harmful content within 24 hours.

Common questions

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, posts, photos and messages.
  • Report a post, comment, message or person: tap ••• on it, or swipe left on a comment, and choose Report.
  • Block someone: from their profile, or from ••• on any of their posts.
  • Make your account private: Profile → Settings → Private account. Only followers you approve see your Sips.
  • Turn off "here now" or notifications: Profile → Settings.
  • Why can't I check in? Check-ins only work when you're at the shop. Make sure location is on for Let's Sip in your iPhone's Settings.

For coffee shops

Want to become a Founding Shop, fix your shop's details, or ask about events? Email support@letssip.app with your shop's name and address.
