Let's Sip Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Let's Sip ("Let's Sip," "we," "us") is a social coffee app, operated by Alexis Becerra. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. This policy explains what we collect, what we don't, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We never see your GPS location. Check-ins record only which coffee shop you're at, and we delete that when you leave or after 30 minutes.
  • Your check-in history is stored only on your iPhone, not on our servers.
  • Shop chat messages are deleted after 24 hours.
  • We remove location and camera details from photos on your phone before they're uploaded.
  • Photos are automatically checked for nudity, violence and hate symbols before anyone can see them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don't sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything you've posted from within the app, at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address (used only to sign you in and for important account messages), plus the display name and username you choose. You can optionally add a profile photo, a short bio and a home city.

Things you post. Sips (a photo, caption, drink type and the shop you tag, along with that shop's location rounded to about 1 kilometer so Sips can appear in the "Nearby" feed), comments, likes, community tags for shops (like "WiFi" or "Study"), shop chat messages, and "Sip with me" invites (a shop and a time, shared with your mutual friends).

Check-ins. When you check in, we store which coffee shop you're at and when. We keep only your current check-in, and delete it when you check out, when you check in somewhere else, or after 30 minutes, whichever comes first.

Social connections. Who you follow and who follows you, and people you've blocked.

Photo safety checks. Every photo you post in a Sip or use as your profile photo is automatically checked by Amazon Rekognition, a service of Amazon Web Services (AWS), for nudity, sexual content, graphic violence and hate symbols before anyone else can see it. A photo that fails is deleted right away; we keep only a note of which categories it matched (not the photo) for 90 days, so you can ask us to review the decision. For photos that pass, we keep a record that the check passed until you delete your account. Amazon processes each photo only to perform the check, and we've opted out of Amazon using our content to improve its services.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store your iPhone's push notification token so Apple Push Notification service can deliver alerts (such as new followers, "Sip with me" invites and friends checking in nearby). We also keep a short log of which alerts were sent, so you aren't alerted about the same thing twice in a day; it's deleted after 2 days. You can turn alerts off in Settings or in your iPhone's Settings.

Spam prevention. To limit fake check-ins and spam, we keep the time of each recent check-in (not the place) for about 2 hours, and limit how often accounts can post, chat, follow and upload.

Reports and moderation. If you report content, we store the report, including a copy of the reported text and which account it's about, so we can review it (even after a chat message expires). The person you report isn't told who reported them. If an account is suspended for breaking our community guidelines, we keep a record of the suspension until the account is deleted.

Technical logs. Our hosting provider automatically keeps short-lived server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate and secure the service. They're deleted after about 7 days.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your GPS location. To check you in, your iPhone compares its own location with the shop's location on the device. Your coordinates are never stored by us. To show the "Nearby" feed, the app sends an approximate location (rounded to about 1 kilometer) with the request; it's used only to find nearby Sips and isn't saved.
  • Photo metadata. Before a photo leaves your phone, the app removes its embedded data (such as GPS coordinates, camera model and timestamps) and resizes it.
  • Your contacts, advertising identifier or browsing activity.
  • Analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

3. Information stored only on your device

Your check-in history, and the badges calculated from it, stay on your iPhone in encrypted storage. We never receive them. Deleting the app or your account removes them.

4. How we use information

We use your information only to run Let's Sip:

  • to sign you in
  • to show your posts, check-ins and chat messages to the people allowed to see them
  • to show shops' anonymous "here now" counts
  • to keep the community safe, by reviewing reports and enforcing blocks
  • to respond to you

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

5. Who can see what

  • "Here now": by default, other people see only an anonymous count of people at a shop. Your name appears only if you choose "Friends" (people who mutually follow you) or "Everyone" in Settings → Privacy.
  • Sips: visible to signed-in Let's Sip users, including in the "Nearby" feed of people near the shop you tagged, or only to followers you approve if you turn on Private account. A Sip reported by several people may be hidden until we review it.
  • Shop chats: anyone can read a shop's chat, but people who aren't signed in only ever see aliases like "Guest ☕️ 42", never names. You can also choose to always chat under an alias.
  • "Friends here now": if you share your name in "here now" (Friends or Everyone), your mutual friends who have this alert on get a notification like "Alex is at Augie's Coffee" when you check in. Choose "Off" to never send it.
  • "Sip with me": your invite is visible to your mutual friends for 12 hours, along with who has said they're in.
  • Blocking: people you block can't see your profile, Sips, chat messages or your name in "here now," and you won't see theirs.
  • Your city can be hidden from your profile.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage in the United States. It processes data only on our behalf.
  • Apple Maps provides coffee shop listings and maps. To show nearby shops, the app asks Apple for places in an area around you, rounded to about 1 kilometer. Apple handles these requests under the Apple Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this.
  • Sign in with Apple lets you hide your real email address. When you delete your account, we ask Apple to revoke Let's Sip's access to your Apple Account.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) checks photos for harmful content with Amazon Rekognition (see "Photo safety checks" above) and sends our account emails, such as email confirmation and password reset, with Amazon Simple Email Service. AWS processes this data only on our behalf, in the United States.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications to your iPhone.
  • Cloudflare hosts our website (letssip.app) and, like any web host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect it.
  • Microsoft 365 hosts our support email. If you email us, your message is stored there.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change what's visible: in Settings → Privacy.
  • Edit your profile: in Settings.
  • Delete a Sip: at any time.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you've posted from our servers, and clears your visit history from your phone.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us at support@letssip.app and we'll send it within 30 days.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, and to access, correct and delete it, without being treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law. To make a request, email support@letssip.app. We may need to confirm it's your account before acting on the request.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access to our database is restricted by security rules that enforce the privacy settings above. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit the risk by keeping as little data as we can.

10. Children

You must be 13 or older to use Let's Sip. It is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If we make significant changes, we'll update the effective date and let you know in the app before the changes take effect.
